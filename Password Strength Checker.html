          Password guide

          How to create a better password

          • Use 12 or more characters. Longer is stronger.
          • Try a passphrase: 4 random words, a number and a symbol.
          • Mix uppercase, lowercase, numbers and symbols.
          • Use a different password for every account.
          • Keep them in a free password manager such as Bitwarden.

          What to avoid

          • Names, birthdays, phone numbers and pet names.
          • Common choices like password, 123456 and qwerty.
          • Repeats and patterns such as aaaa, 1234 and abcd.
          • Simple swaps like p@ssw0rd. Attackers know them.
          • Reusing one password on many sites.

          How to prevent password theft

          • Turn on two-factor authentication (a code sent to your phone).
          • Don't click links in unexpected emails or messages. Fake login pages steal passwords.
          • Never share a password or a code, even with someone who says they are support.
          • Avoid logging in on public Wi-Fi or shared computers.
          • Check haveibeenpwned.com to see if your email appeared in a data leak, and change the password if it did.
          Everything runs inside your browser. No password is ever sent anywhere.
